Inside StudyStep.

Starting material
Preview an English Wikipedia introduction, or import PDF, DOCX, TXT, Markdown, or pasted notes.
Personal workspace
Your active study set, sources, and progress are saved locally. Backups and shareable exports support reuse.
Local AI
Selected passages inform an Ollama draft. Citation and structure checks, model review, and card-by-card approval keep the source close.
Study tools
Study guides, flashcards, multiple-choice questions, recall practice, and scheduled review.
Focus & routine
A focus timer, credits for collection designs, and a study journal record time spent learning.
Data
Study files stay on your computer. Topic queries go to English Wikipedia; optional model generation runs locally.
