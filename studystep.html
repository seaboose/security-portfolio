Inside StudyStep.

Study material
Import a document, paste notes, or use a Wikipedia introduction. Selected passages can become a study guide, flashcards, and practice questions.
Optional local AI
Ollama drafts cards from your selected passages. Inspect each quotation and edit the draft before saving.
Source updates
Follow an article and choose a check frequency. StudyStep checks while open and lets you review changes when you return.
Related topics
See why a suggested article fits, then accept it for your reading list or dismiss it.
Topic assessment
Use your practice results or mark a topic “Needs practice” or “Confident” to guide later activities.
Storage
Your study material and progress are saved on your computer. Topic searches and source checks contact English Wikipedia.
