Made with Natural Earth.

Local modifications: feature properties removed; coordinates rounded to four
decimal places. A regular 2-degree grid of land-contained points was derived
from the polygon geometry for a dotted map display. Dots cover latitude -59
through 83 degrees and longitude -179 through 179 degrees; Antarctica is
therefore omitted from this optional dot display. All original land polygon
geometry, including Antarctica, remains in features.

world-land.json schema:
{
  "type": "FeatureCollection",
  "features": [{"type":"Feature", "properties":{}, "geometry":{
    "type":"Polygon" or "MultiPolygon", "coordinates":GeoJSON coordinates
  }}],
  "dots": [[longitude, latitude], ...],
  "dotSpacingDegrees": 2,
  "dotLatitudeRange": [-59,83]
}
Coordinates are decimal degrees in GeoJSON longitude/latitude order.

This asset contains geographic shapes only. Animated attack arcs and endpoint
activity in the portfolio are illustrative, not a real-time threat feed.
